# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (pre-release)

Release date pending. This initial version is a public-source macro-financial
screening prototype, not a validated U.S. bank early-warning model.

- Use direct browser requests for World Bank and Frankfurter public APIs.
- Default the risk overview to the United States and prioritize banking
  indicators in the monitor.
- Remove the unsupported AI analyst chat and fixed, unbenchmarked scenario
  adjustments.
- Add project authorship, a data dictionary, methodology documentation, and
  explicit model-status disclosures.
- Add author, date, and method to the downloadable risk brief.

The FDIC/FFIEC bank model, 2023 back-test, FRED/ALFRED policy forecasts, SHAP
analysis, DOI, and open-source license are not included in this release.
